I'm a product leader who works on cloud infrastructure, data platforms, and
security. I like the layers of software that other software depends on.

Most recently I was Director of Product Management at
[Saviynt](https://saviynt.com/), an enterprise identity security company, where
I led product execution for its FedRAMP High authorization on AWS GovCloud.
Before that I was a Senior Product Manager at
[InfluxData](https://www.influxdata.com/), working on its multi-cloud
time-series database service.

I came to product by way of support and partner engineering. I hold an MBA from
Berkeley Haas and live in the San Francisco Bay Area.
